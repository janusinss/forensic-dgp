About this workflow

Conditioned SFT V2 (p2_u0224) restores visible information. Its Base Model, U96 Adaptation, Spatial Head and V2 Components work together as one restoration model. Native CCTV usefulness and final acceptance remain under review. Completion is a separate optional step.

Request a clearer or less-covered crop when visible facial structure cannot be meaningfully inspected. A plausible sharper face is not proof of recovered identity.

Input preparation

Globally soft, unknown or mixed inputs can bypass. Surroundings blur helped some examples but was mixed or adverse in the paired study. Automatic blur detection and matching remain unavailable.

Manual Gaussian blur starts at radius 1. The preview adds an 8-pixel protection margin and a 16-pixel outside transition entirely within Apply. It rebuilds from the original; previews never accumulate blur. No arbitrary noise, JPEG or mosaic is copied.

Every protected native pixel and every pixel outside Apply stays unchanged during preparation. Generation can still change facial appearance.
