About this workflow

This app tries to improve a face picture. Its results are experimental and are still being reviewed. Removing a covering is an optional extra step.

Use a clearer or less-covered picture if the face is hard to see. A sharper face is not proof of identity.

Prepare the picture

Bypass keeps the original picture. You can optionally blur selected background areas, but this did not help every picture and may make some worse.

You choose how much blur to apply. The preview includes a small safety margin around protected areas and softens the edge of the selected area. Each preview starts from the original picture.

Protected areas and areas outside your blur selection stay unchanged during preparation. The generated face may still look different.
