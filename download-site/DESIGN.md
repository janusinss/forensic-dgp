# Download landing visual direction

Use the incumbent Forensic DGP identity. This expands the application brand onto
a simple public download surface; it does not replace the application's design.

## Visual authority
App tokens and original speech-block composition are authoritative. Apple-like
restraint is a supporting hierarchy reference, not a new palette or component kit.

Canvas #f7f9f5; white surface; ink #172725; muted #4b6056; accent #176b52;
dark forest #103c30; borders #cad6ce. Local Inter, with system sans fallback.
Spacing follows a 4px base. No decorative glow, floating pills, fake terminal,
photographic faces, invented metrics or testimonials.

## Spatial thesis
The product purpose and primary download lead on the left. Branded speech shapes
on the right provide continuity with the app, without implying a private image
or a model result. A compact three-step setup strip follows. Requirements stay
near the download; preview and identity limits remain visible.

Desktop maximum width1120px; heading about72px over2lines. At narrow widths,
stack the hero in reading order. Header remains below80px. Natural page scrolling
is allowed. Avoid nested scrolling and horizontal overflow.

## States and craft
Use >=44px targets, keyboard-visible2px focus rings, quiet200ms hover/press
transitions, and reduced-motion overrides. Clipboard success/failure is announced.
No fabricated download progress. Keep the image-free visual lightweight.
