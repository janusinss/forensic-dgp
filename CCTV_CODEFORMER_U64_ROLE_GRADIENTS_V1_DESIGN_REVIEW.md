## Why this is the next step

The [audited continuation](CCTV_CODEFORMER_U64_CLEANANCHORS_CONTINUATION_V1_RETURN_REVIEW.md)
raised global PNG adjacent-error gain from 1.627216% to 2.317733% against frozen
U96 on eight adaptive TRAIN monitor photographs. Clear photographic MAE worsened
12.4691%, global-blur MAE 10.6817%, and local-center MAE 8.5327%; dark MAE improved
7.9366%. Its scheduled preservation stop at eight updates remains binding. Do not
resume it, select an earlier stopped checkpoint, add epochs or loosen its gates.

The saved-array diagnosis establishes a tradeoff, not its unique cause. Every
clear monitor has better adjacent-error scores and worse photographic MAE.
Constant RGB offsets are invisible to adjacent differences; however, tone alone
does not explain every case. Clear central128 MAE also worsens. Joint accumulated
gradients cannot reveal which separate role or objective supplied the direction.

The current post-fusion heads already use observed encoder, decoder, fused and
coarse features. Adding generic conditioning would not establish a distinct
solution. Prior brightness routing and scalar correction trials also retain their
failures. Raising learning rate or extending the failed recipe is not justified.

photographic fidelity, and do gradients from one role oppose another role's
fidelity direction? Measure this before choosing a weighting or architecture
change. No gradient direction is applied to parameters.

Use the same 16 admitted optimization photographs and all four correlated roles
for each photograph: localized Gaussian blur, global Gaussian blur, clear, and
linear low light. These are **64 paired TRAIN cases**, not 64 independent people.
Keep the eight adaptive monitor photographs and every legacy guard out of gradient
queries. Native CCTV, wild stress images and reserved FINAL stay out entirely.
This matched-photo diagnostic differs from the stopped worker's offset-photo

For each of the 64 cases, save one full forward and three separate gradients of
the same live graph with respect to the existing 36 heads: the exact stopped

adjacent-residual error. MAE and adjacent scopes are central128 for the local
role and whole256 for the others. Save complete vectors, not only their norms.
Zero or unused directions are recorded explicitly; a zero norm has undefined
cosine and does not become a fabricated perfect alignment.

Four historical initial-u64 RAW/PNG witnesses and four same-candidate repeats add

## How the evidence may guide a later pilot

Report per-photograph and aggregate norms, dots, cosine where defined, and local
directional derivatives. Separately evaluate the hypothetical fresh-Adam-shaped
first-step vector by array arithmetic; cosine of raw gradients alone does not
represent Adam. This calculation constructs no optimizer and changes no model.
Its derivatives are local RAW predictions, not measured finite steps, delivered
PNG gradients, a chosen update, or proof of preservation.

If one role opposes photographic fidelity across photographs, a later design can
investigate that specific interference or objective mismatch. If the initial
directions agree, do not force a conflict explanation: investigate finite-step
curvature, sampling, clipping or later-state behavior instead. Undefined or mixed
evidence remains inconclusive. No automatic PCGrad, reweighting, new branch,
learning-rate selection, checkpoint promotion or next training launch follows.

All actual forwards and gradient queries stay in the user's manual tmux workflow
on the L4 VM. The new stage reads the completed parent stage without changing its
files. The existing parent initializer's exact cwd/source guards remain intact.
Local preparation uses only source, file and NumPy checks. Runtime caps are 900
worker seconds, 960 externally plus at most 20 termination seconds, 180 per phase,

## Primary research and its limits

CodeFormer's official implementation combines a codebook decoder with SFT
feature fusion and AdaIN. It supports investigating the existing fusion path,
but does not demonstrate that our added heads or current data are adequate.
[Official architecture source](https://github.com/sczhou/CodeFormer/blob/master/basicsr/archs/codeformer_arch.py).

Gradient Surgery studies task-gradient interference. It motivates measuring
separate directions; a negative cosine alone does not establish all conditions
for harmful training or justify applying its projection method here.
[Yu et al., NeurIPS 2020](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/hash/3fe78a8acf5fda99de95303940a2420c-Abstract.html).

AirNet and PromptIR demonstrate input-guided handling of several degradations in
their own benchmarks. Their results do not establish CCTV face identity fidelity,
low-light improvement, or the need for another conditioning branch in our already
conditioned u64. They remain architectural context, not substitute evidence.
[AirNet, CVPR 2022](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Li_All-in-One_Image_Restoration_for_Unknown_Corruption_CVPR_2022_paper.html),
[PromptIR, NeurIPS 2023](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/e187897ed7780a579a0d76fd4a35d107-Paper-Conference.pdf).
