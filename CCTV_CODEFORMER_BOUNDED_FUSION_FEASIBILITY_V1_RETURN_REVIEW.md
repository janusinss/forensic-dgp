## What the images and measurements show

These are mean per-case scores on the identical 16 normal and 16 dark TRAIN
observations. Lower RGB MAE and higher SSIM are descriptor directions, not
identity or usefulness judgments. They use the delivered PNGs, direct clean256
targets, and the declared RGB seven-window SSIM formula. Native512 and RAW256
measurements are retained separately in the linked scientific records.

The earlier training has real benefits: stopped FP32 improves this cohort's
normal mean SSIM and dark MAE/SSIM against original CodeFormer. Several severe
examples have fewer coarse invented facial details. Its excess normal
brightening and smoothing remain. The new interventions retain some benefits
but do not improve every relevant property together.

Relative to the constant bound, the fixed gate lowers normal MAE on 15/16
observations and lowers mean absolute luminance-bias error by 0.008791. It also
raises dark MAE on 13/16 observations, with three exact ties, and raises mean
dark bias error by 0.006587. SSIM is lower on 11/16 normal and 12/16 dark
observations. Central-gradient vector error rises on all 16 normal observations
and 13 dark observations. SSIM and gradient descriptors are not pure measures
of facial geometry; tone and texture contribute to them.

The constant bound itself costs dark improvement relative to stopped FP32:
dark mean MAE rises by 0.009513 and SSIM falls by 0.008225. Therefore the constant
bound is retained as evidence, not automatically chosen as the next trained
architecture. Original-fusion rollback makes that dark cost larger.

The two prospectively fixed overlap stress cases explain why a brightness
scalar is insufficient:

|Darkest normal: `train_u0011_slot1`|0.991255|Bias error barely changes, 0.042945→0.042628; it remains above original's 0.002068|

Absolute image brightness does not identify whether additional exposure
correction is needed. This is a result on the fixed TRAIN cohort, not a
population classifier error or an ethnicity statement. Neither changing the
gate thresholds/beta after seeing these images nor appending epochs to the
failed recipe follows from this result.

## Root visual findings and scope

Across the complete sheets, the fixed gate mainly adjusts tone relative to the
constant bound. Several normally exposed results retain more contrast and less
smoothing than stopped FP32. Dark counterparts often lose useful visibility.
Clear glasses remain visible in the sampled glasses cases. The two fixed stress
cases show the limitation directly rather than a consistent visible advantage
for brightness gating.

Severe facial-detail failures remain. Pair 08 retains mismatched facial outline
and eye/cheek detail. Pair 14 retains eyelid/expression and hairstyle differences
against its paired photograph. The strongly turned scope-challenge face in pair
10 has closed eyelids in the clean target but an opened visible eye in the
restored images. Photometric changes do not solve these problems. The intended
app must request a clearer or more frontal crop when information is insufficient;
these outputs do not establish recovered identity.

The four `clear_normal` TRAIN controls fall within the existing 0.01 mean / 0.03
per-case MAE-regression values when shown descriptively against original.
**Those four observations are not the full 61-clear/53-normal-blur development
guards.** This diagnostic does not override the earlier three clear failures,
qualify a model, establish native CCTV performance or test reserved FINAL
identities. The retained PNG/255 inputs match the canonical training pixel
values: the original camera renderer also produces RGB8 before normalization.
This32-case frozen-state inference experiment is still not a replay of the
full200 training forwards or their gradient/update sequence. Earlier frozen
documents/protocol metadata describing original inputs as continuous are a
documentation error; all historical bytes remain retained. The independent
first200 pixel hashes and unchanged regeneration are the authoritative control.

## Decision and retained evidence

Reject this simple observed-brightness gate as an unchanged next training
treatment, following its prospective overlap-stress decision rule. Keep the
bounded and rollback evidence and both original checkpoints. The next design
must address the demonstrated preservation/visibility conflict without routing
from clean targets, synthetic profile labels or source identities. Actual
training still requires a separately verified finite manual L4/tmux packet.
