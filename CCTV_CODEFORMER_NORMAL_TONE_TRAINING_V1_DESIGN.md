Only the native coarse loss for known normal TRAIN slots1/3 changes. For each
observation independently:

```text
P = average_pool2d(kernel=32, stride=32)  # native512 ->16x16
e = P((native_prediction.float()+1)/2) - P(clean_RGB512.float())
```
