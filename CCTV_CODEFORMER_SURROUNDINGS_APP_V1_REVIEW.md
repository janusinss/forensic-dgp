21 October 2026. [Open the experimental page](http://127.0.0.1:61815/restoration-context-test). The existing [normal comparison](http://127.0.0.1:61815/restoration-spatial-test) remains available. This is a usable assisted preprocessing experiment, not a newly trained model or an automatic blur detector. Main-app defaults and model qualification remain unchanged.

The user can upload one crop, draw the complete face/blur protection rectangle or enter native pixel bounds, preview the actual protected input, confirm the area and compare the same model on original versus prepared inputs. The page offers R2 or untouched CodeFormer. It refuses unreadable/out-of-scope input, unconfirmed or stale previews, invalid bounds and unsupported selections before any model call. Drawing, numeric edits or a new upload invalidate the earlier preview and confirmation. A center rectangle is only an editing starting point. The reviewed wild5 preset is available solely for its exact retained original bytes; it cannot be silently applied to another image.
