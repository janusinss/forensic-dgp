# User feedback on brightness — 15 October 2026

The user said during the independent V2 returned-result review:

> somehow i do agree increasing the brightness for much clearer face.

The current restoration preference therefore allows moderate brightening when
it improves the visibility of a dark face. Brightness increase alone is not a
reason to discard an otherwise useful result. Preserve visible facial features,
natural contrast and readable detail; avoid clipping, washed-out regions and
unsupported changes to eyes, brows, nose, mouth, expression, glasses or jaw.

This is a preference about helpful brightness. It is not a statement that the
user reviewed every V2 output, accepted V2 as the application model, waived
appearance preservation, or approved exact recovery of hidden identity.

V2's predeclared clear and normal-blur photometric guard failures remain recorded
exactly. They must not be rewritten as passes. A future design may distinguish
exposure changes from facial-structure preservation prospectively, with separately
reported delivered pixels and predeclared criteria. Do not derive a new passing
threshold from the three failed development faces or apply hidden exposure
correction to an output before reporting its original metrics.

Restoration remains the immediate focus. Completion and covering-detector model
development remain deferred until restoration is accepted.
