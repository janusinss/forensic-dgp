1. CORE UNDERSTANDING: WHAT WE ARE DOING & WHY
================================================================================

1.1 The Municipal CCTV Problem in Zamboanga City
Municipal surveillance cameras in Zamboanga City frequently capture critical 
incidents under severe environmental and physical degradation:
  - Low Optical Resolution: Suspect faces often occupy fewer than 32x32 pixels.
  - Aggressive Compression: NVR feeds starved for bandwidth produce severe H.264 
    block-edge macroblocking.
  - Environmental Degradation: Tropical humidity, torrential rain scattering, and 
    backlight glare reduce contrast.
  - Hardware Motion & Defocus: Rapid subject transit causes directional motion blur, 
    and fixed-focal lenses produce heavy optical defocus.
  - Analog Interlacing: Coaxial CCTV signals exhibit comb scanlines (NTSC/PAL 480i/576i).

1.2 The Forensic Non-Hallucination Constraint
Generic super-resolution tools (e.g., standard ESRGAN, CodeFormer, Stable Diffusion) 
generate visually pleasing results by hallucinating artificial biometric features. 
Under the Philippine Rules on Electronic Evidence (A.M. No. 01-7-01-SC):
  - Fabricating biometric details destroys the digital chain of custody.
  - Hallucinated facial features render surveillance footage legally inadmissible in court.
  - The reconstruction MUST remain mathematically anchored to the true Euclidean 
    geometry of the suspect recorded by the camera sensor.

1.3 Our Solution: Morphologically Constrained DGP
We employ a Deep Generative Prior (DGP) conditioned by:
  - A MobileNetV2 Feature Pyramid Network (FPN) generator.
  - A 68-point Morphological FAN Loss (Euclidean distance on facial landmarks) 
    that strictly penalizes biometric drift.
  - Top-K Probabilistic Sampling: Instead of forcing a single deterministic guess, 
    the system provides investigators with ranked candidates (Rank 1: Best Fit, 
    Rank 2: Edge Focus, Rank 3: Natural Tone) with explicit loss metrics.

2. THE RECONSTRUCTION PIPELINE (HOW INFERENCE WORKS)
================================================================================

When an investigator inputs an image into the system, it processes through 5 stages:

Stage 1: Blind Forensic Signal Pre-Conditioning (degradation.py)
  - Interlacing Comb Detection: Measures vertical vs. horizontal high-frequency energy. 
    If comb artifacts are detected, vertical field interpolation dissolves scanlines.
  - Mosaic Anti-Aliasing: Detects zero-gradient block boundaries from pixelation / 
    censorship and applies localized anti-aliasing.
  - Sensor Denoising: Estimates Gaussian noise standard deviation (sigma) and applies 
    edge-preserving bilateral filtering.

Stage 2: 3-Tier Canonical Face Registration (degradation.py)
  - Tier 1 (5-Point FAN Alignment): Detects eyes, nose tip, and mouth corners. Warps 
    the face via cv2.estimateAffinePartial2D (uniform scale + rotation + translation) 
    into standard FFHQ coordinates (inter-ocular distance = 66.0 px in a 256x256 frame).
  - Tier 2 (Anti-Aliased Fallback): Runs landmark detection on smoothed gradients if 
    pixelation prevents immediate detection.
  - Tier 3 (Morphological Center-Crop): Square aspect-ratio crop for extreme blur.

Stage 3: Generative Restoration (models/dgp_synthesizer.py)
  - Normalized input tensor is processed through the MobileNetV2 FPN backbone.
  - Generates enhanced 256x256 facial representations matching the learned prior.

Stage 4: Top-K Forensic Sampling & Illumination Calibration (app.py)
  - Produces Rank 1 (Balanced Synthesis), Rank 2 (High-Definition Edge Focus), and 
    Rank 3 (Soft Denoised Tone).
  - Adaptive LAB illumination calibration: Matches the output luminance dynamic range 
    to the source input, preventing shadow-cast luminance collapse.

Stage 5: Seamless In-Context Paste-Back (degradation.py)
  - Computes the inverse affine matrix (M^-1) and seamlessly blends the restored 
    face back into the original wide surveillance frame using elliptical feathering.

3. TRAINING ENVIRONMENT & DATASET CURRICULUM (GOOGLE COLAB / VM)
================================================================================

All heavy model training is executed inside Google Colab / Cloud Compute Engine VMs 
equipped with NVIDIA GPUs, while model checkpoints are transferred to the local 
repository for testing and evaluation.

3.1 Datasets Used in Google Colab / VM
  1. FFHQ Dataset: 70,000 high-resolution diverse facial portraits downsampled to 128x128.
  2. Asian Face Dataset: 10,000 real Asian face portraits extracted from Hugging Face 
     (hiennguyen9874/face-age-gender-asian) to inject accurate demographic priors 
     representative of the Zamboanga City population.

3.2 Training Phases Completed
  - Phase 1 (Epochs 1 -> 10):
      First-order Gaussian blur and 4x downsampling on FFHQ thumbnails.
      Checkpoint: checkpoints/dgp_improved_epoch_10.pth
  - Phase 2 (Epochs 11 -> 20):
      Photometric refinement with learning rate annealing (1e-4 -> 1e-6).
      Checkpoint: checkpoints/dgp_improved_epoch_20.pth
      Peak Metrics: 25.01 dB PSNR | 0.7862 SSIM | 0.4896 ArcFace
  - Phase 3 (Epochs 21 -> 26) [Completed]:
      Multi-directory training (FFHQ + 10,000 Asian faces) under second-order 
      compound CCTV degradations (motion blur, thermal noise, atmospheric haze, 
      interlacing comb injection, lossy H.264 codec quantization).
      Output Checkpoint: checkpoints/dgp_zamboanga_final.pth


================================================================================
4. KEY DIAGNOSTICS & WHAT WAS RESOLVED
================================================================================

4.1 The Canonical Template Resolution Discrepancy (Fixed)
  - Issue: Synthetic test images initially produced squashed, blurry pupils in the UI.
  - Root Cause: degradation.py had an artificial 102.4 px eye-distance template 
    (1.6x over-zoom). This forced facial landmarks outside the generator's learned 
    convolutional receptive fields.
  - Solution: Re-anchored dst_pts to standard FFHQ proportions (66.0 px eye distance). 
    Sharp irises, round pupils, and natural facial framing were restored.

4.2 Test Suite Modernization
  - Synthetic Data (Database-Derived): test_images/synthetic_data_1.png through 10.png 
    simulating specific municipal degradation profiles (motion, haze, sensor noise, 
    sub-32 downsampling, NVR compression, analog interlacing).
  - Wild Data (Real Surveillance): test_images/wild1.png through 5.png representing 
    unconstrained real CCTV captures.


================================================================================
5. QUALITY AUDIT: WHY `wild5.png` APPEARS BLURRY
================================================================================

5.1 The Diagnosis
When testing wild5.png, the output remains soft:
  - wild5.png contains artificial censorship-level Gaussian blur (sigma approx 18 to 22 px), 
    completely eliminating high-frequency phase and gradient information.
  - In dataset.py, training blur was bounded at sigma <= 3.0 and displacement d <= 10.
  - Under Charbonnier/L1 loss, when out-of-distribution blur with zero remaining gradient 
    is fed into the network, the mathematically optimal loss minimization produces the 
    smooth conditional average (conditional mean).

5.2 The Non-Hallucination Balance
Under forensic admissibility rules, if the camera captured zero eye signal (pure blur), 
an algorithm that draws sharp, detailed eyes from thin air is fabricating evidence. 
However, the generative prior CAN be strengthened to recover structural contours 
more assertively.

6. HOW WE ARE IMPROVING THE SYSTEM (CONCRETE ACTION PATHS)
================================================================================

We have two proven, complementary paths to achieve higher reconstruction quality:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PATH A: Test-Time Iterative Recurrent Pass (Immediate / In Web UI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Concept: When an input has severe degradation (Laplacian variance < 40), feed the 
    output of Pass 1 back into the generator for a second refinement pass:
        Pass 1 -> Latent Frequency Boost -> Pass 2 (Recurrent Refinement)
  - Experimental Result: Tested in scratch/wild5_iterative_solution.png. Pass 2 successfully 
    separated the eye sockets, formed pupil contours, and defined the nose bridge 
    and lips without hallucinating fake identity.
  - Implementation: Built directly into app.py / generate_top_k(). Zero cloud GPU cost.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PATH B: Phase 4 Fine-Tuning in Google Colab / Cloud VM (Defocus Inversion)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Goal: Train the model to invert heavy optical defocus and heavy blur explicitly.
  - Changes to dataset.py:
      Expand Gaussian blur sigma from [0.8, 3.0] -> [1.0, 10.0]
      Expand motion blur displacement d from [2, 10] -> [4, 20]
  - Changes to train.py:
      Increase lambda_sobel (edge gradient loss) from 0.5 -> 1.5 (forces sharp edges)
      Increase lambda_fft (frequency domain loss) from 0.1 -> 0.4 (penalizes blur)
  - Google Colab / VM Execution:
      Resume from checkpoints/dgp_zamboanga_final.pth for 4-5 epochs (~12 hours).
      Export the new weights as checkpoints/dgp_zamboanga_v2.pth.
