## What the signals show

The PNG custom operation's forward values exactly equal the actual saved PNG
decoded to FP32. Its identity backward remains an explicitly **biased surrogate**,
not the true derivative through discrete floor conversion.

The pooled RAW-versus-PNG-surrogate cosines are:

| TRAIN role | MAE | Adjacent error |
|---|---:|---:|
| Local Gaussian, central128 | 0.994518 | 0.999853 |
| Global Gaussian, whole256 | 0.995786 | 0.999377 |
| Clear, whole256 | 0.992757 | 0.999705 |
| Low light, whole256 | 0.999759 | 0.999347 |

All 128 same-role per-photo RAW/PNG comparisons are positive. The surrogate
therefore largely follows the existing RAW signal at this original-u64 point.
The role conflict remains: pooled low-light MAE opposes local/global/clear MAE
in PNG-surrogate space, with cosines approximately -0.7370/-0.9158/-0.8627.
These are gradient relationships on admitted TRAIN photographs, not image
quality scores or evidence about unseen CCTV identities.

## The saved-array feasibility check prevents an unsupported rerun

A separate, local NumPy-only analysis used the already saved vectors. It did not
execute a model, form new autograd queries, modify a parameter or connect to the
VM. It retained all 16 role/domain/metric vectors, normalized after pooling and
examined all 65,535 nonempty faces of their simplex. Synthetic known-solution
checks and an actual KKT certificate support its bounded numerical result.

The original rejected pilot's eight-RAW direction **already has negative dots
against all 16 pooled RAW and PNG-surrogate signals**. Its minimum normalized
margin is 0.17981636. The full-16 solution has margin 0.18201706 and cosine
**0.99999338198** with that prior direction. Under the full-16 direction, 233
individual query dots are negative and 23 positive; pooled descent does not
establish preservation of every photograph.

Omitting these identity-surrogate vectors is therefore not a demonstrated cause
of the finite PNG failure. A near-identical full-16 training pilot would not
test a materially different corrective mechanism. **No such pilot is prepared
or authorized to auto-run.** The old update-one state remains unselected, and
the failed update-two state remains failed.

The previous return already established sparse one-code PNG crossings at the
failing monitor endpoint despite decreasing RAW error. Current results add that
favorable surrogate predictions would also have existed for the original
direction. They do not isolate every contribution of finite displacement,
individual-case variation and optimization-versus-monitor transfer. The current
archive has no new finite displacement at all.

## Next work

The [next design](CCTV_CODEFORMER_U64_PNG_CHECKED_STEP_V1_DESIGN.md) changes how
a finite displacement is selected: require actual delivered-PNG and RAW checks
on the optimization cohort before the unchanged monitor/guard screen. It does
not merely add nearly duplicate gradients. The design is prospective and needs
source implementation, tests, independent review and a verified manual packet;
**there is no next launch guide yet**. Do not rerun this completed diagnostic.

The straight-through estimator is a heuristic with problem-specific limits;
[Yin et al., ICLR 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.05662) analyze a particular
binarized network, not CCTV restoration. [Sener and Koltun, NeurIPS 2018](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2018/hash/432aca3a1e345e339f35a30c8f65edce-Abstract.html)
motivate gradient-based multi-objective optimization. Neither source certifies
our finite PNG changes or restoration usefulness. The decisions here follow
the retained measurements rather than transferring those papers' guarantees.
