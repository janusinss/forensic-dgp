## Why the final check failed

Four fixed coordinate caps were tested independently from original u64:
`2e-5`, `1.5e-5`, `1e-5`, and `5e-6`. All four 64-case TRAIN banks completed
before selection. Trials 0, 1 and 2 met the specified optimization role-mean
RAW/PNG requirements; trial 3 did not. The fixed first-eligible rule correctly
selected trial 0. Monitors and guards were excluded from that selection.

On the selected trial's 56-case final bank, the inherited requirements,
original terminal requirements, and four strict clear/low-light MAE checks
passed. One additional role-mean endpoint failed: localized-blur central
128-pixel **PNG adjacent RGB error**. It appears twice in the retained gate
because both the original and prior references are the same initial state;
these are two failed comparisons of one endpoint, not two separate defects.

| Final localized-blur monitor endpoint | Original u64 | Selected trial 0 | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| PNG adjacent RGB error | 0.01697094396257641 | 0.01697106960719266 | +1.2564461624939294e-7 |
| RAW adjacent RGB error | 0.01696742769701057 | 0.01696728013405898 | -1.4756295159054633e-7 |

Lower is better. PNG error increased **0.0007403514% relative to original**.
This tiny error increase is not a new structure-gain percentage, nor a loss of
the original u64's historical 1.627% global PNG gain against its earlier
reference. The unchanged zero-increase requirement rejects the candidate.

All eight localized monitor cases improved this RAW endpoint; five worsened
its PNG counterpart. Saved integer pixels explain the difference: 882 of
393,216 central RGB components changed, yielding +1,752 increased minus 1,727
decreased absolute target-edge residual units, a net +25. Integer prediction
and actual FP32 metric delta differ by only 2.94e-13. This is a reproduced
quantized-output effect, not an archive error or a reason to alter the gate.
The TRAIN localized PNG mean improved by 4.77448863813e-8, while the separate
monitor mean worsened. Passing TRAIN selection did not establish transfer.

Individual increases remain visible in the audit: selected trial 0 worsened
56 of 192 available photographic metric-by-case rows across 30 case IDs.
These are metric rows, not a claim that 30 entire photos are unusable. Trials
1 and 2 have no final monitor qualification; retrospective fallback is forbidden.

## Next decision before another model change

anchor continuation, the two-update gate-aligned route, and this actual-PNG
finite selection test. The assumption that more careful small steps alone
would produce a useful preserved successor is unsupported. Under
`AGENTS.md`'s three-strike rule, model/training changes stop at the last working
state while one diagnostic choice is pending: review **training examples and
loss design** first, or review the **trainable reconstruction architecture**.
The recommended first review is training examples/loss design; it is a design
discussion, not authorization to rerun or prepare another near-identical pilot.
