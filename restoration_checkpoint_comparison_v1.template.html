3. Decide which model deserves a training pilot

Choose a checkpoint that preserves the visible face and gives useful results across both CCTV sources. A candidate still needs a justified finite VM pilot, preservation checks and a separate final evaluation before it becomes the app’s primary restorer.

QMUL-SurvFace and ChokePoint are reported separately. Tiny or unreadable crops remain visible here so invented details are not mistaken for recovered structure. Historical training overlap is not fully excluded for every old checkpoint. No ethnicity or Zamboanga performance is inferred.
