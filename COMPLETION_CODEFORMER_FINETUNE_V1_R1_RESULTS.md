## Complete visual review and remaining limitations

All 16 complete sheets were inspected at original resolution: 128 monitor outputs across four snapshots and 64 original/trained public-development outputs. The 32 public cases are unpaired original/degraded photographs; their historical native filename suffix does not make them CCTV.

Several mask and object estimates are plausible, and some paired expression/skin blends improve modestly. Useful completion is not universal. In the degraded adult-sunglasses example, R1 generates clear frames inside the removed area. Scarf/glove cases retain beard/cloth-like artifacts. Grayscale hand/hair examples gain blue or colored generated regions. Synthetic eye-band targets with glasses are not faithfully reconstructed. True hidden gaze, mouth expression and beard cannot be verified without aligned references. Exact copied pixels do not establish plausible anatomy.

Mask geometry stayed fixed. Unremoved straps, fingers, cloth or glare outside reviewed support are retained footprint issues and must be distinguished from defects generated inside support. No mask was enlarged to hide those defects. Full covering-family qualification and independent human final review remain incomplete. See root_visual_review.json for every sheet's observations.
