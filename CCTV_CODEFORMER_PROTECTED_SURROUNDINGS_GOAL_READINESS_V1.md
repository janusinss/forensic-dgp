## Current preparation contract

Preparation decodes the immutable original upload for every preview. Protect
wins overlaps with Apply; an outward native guard stays unchanged. A documented
transition starts outside that guard and remains within permitted Apply. Native
protected pixels and pixels outside Apply are explicitly copied from the
original. Repeated previews do not blur the previous preview. The kernel can
read original neighboring protected context even though it cannot write there.

Reviewers can paint Protect and Apply separately, erase/correct, preview and
confirm, or bypass preparation. Changes to the source image, masks or parameters
invalidate the preview identity. The server rebuilds the preparation before
accepting a confirmed manual request. A confirmed preview is permission to use
those prepared pixels, not proof of matched blur or useful generated appearance.

Exact native protection does not imply exact generated pixels. Resizing also
requires a separate boundary qualification: only conservative 256-pixel
interiors whose sampling footprint contains no potentially changed native
pixel have an exact resized-input guarantee. No unchanged resized boundary or
recovered hidden identity is claimed.

## What the paired study actually found

Four DEV photographs each supplied clear, global-blur and local-blur inputs.
Each of the 12 correlated cases ran frozen U96 and u64 with original bypass
and one fixed preparation, producing 48 saved outputs. Runtime completed with
48 known attempts, returns and saves, zero unknown outcomes and no training or
VM calls. The supervisor elapsed 459.279 seconds. Independent retained audits
cover all 240 saved arrays, all 48 PNGs, declared delivery and regional metrics,
plus all 84 rendered panels.

The fixed manual radius was 1.0, guard 8 and feather 16. Synthetic corruption
used Gaussian sigma 1.2. These are different operators, so the study does not
claim exact blur matching. Its observed clear control used direct BILINEAR
1024-to-256 preparation while its photographic target used LANCZOS. This is a
declared DEV-study convention; it differs from the separate 96-input TRAIN
cohort whose clear files exactly equal their targets.

Preparation worsened whole PNG MAE in **11/12 cases for each model**, and PNG
SSIM in 11/12 per model. For localized blur, whole and central PNG MAE worsened
in three of four photographs per model. No original/prepared comparison
improved both whole PNG MAE and SSIM. All four clear and all four global-blur
controls worsened whole PNG MAE. The four-photo dependence and small differences
do not establish a population effect or a usefulness threshold.

All generated protected-region pixels need not be identical: the audit records
changes in every one of the 24 original/prepared model comparisons. That is a
scope warning, not by itself a facial-damage verdict. Apply covered the full
frame here, so the empty outside-Apply set adds no outside-Apply preservation
test; separate functional/source evidence must support that invariant.

Root reinspected all three existing sheets in this continuation and saw no
consistent conspicuous new facial structure from preparation. Outputs remain
similar, with small tone, smoothing and edge differences. Tinted glasses,
frames, reflections and makeup remain visible. This reinspection occurred
after the numbers were known; the original pre-numeric review is retained
separately. Historical pending labels on the immutable sheets are superseded
by the completed review receipts, not edited away.

The earlier seven-photo mild study was mixed/negative. Wild5's visual benefit
remains a case-specific **unpaired** observation without an aligned clean
target; it is not evidence of identity recovery or an automatic matcher. The
studies are not pooled, relabeled or swept to select a favorable strength.

## Consequence for the workflow

Keep manual preparation optional, reviewed and rebuilt from the original.
Globally soft, mixed or uncertain inputs can bypass. Do not double blur an
already protected region, make blur automatic for every image, or copy arbitrary
noise, JPEG artifacts or mosaic. Request a clearer crop when visible structure
is insufficient. The goal's automatic proposal/matching stage remains
unqualified; no new matching algorithm or model pilot is selected here.

and the pending diagnostic choice still bind. No further completion/removal-mask
development starts before restoration acceptance. Native/wild evidence stays

active and incomplete.
