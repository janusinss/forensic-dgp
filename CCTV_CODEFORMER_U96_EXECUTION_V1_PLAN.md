## What counts as success

A useful candidate has clearer visible eyes, brows, nose, mouth and jaw, while retaining expression, glasses and other visible appearance. Brightness remains a separately measured contribution; lower whole-image MAE or a brighter face alone does not establish better facial structure. Use region-aware error/structure diagnostics alongside whole-image paired PSNR/SSIM, clear/normal preservation and coded visual comparisons. Preserve existing gates and explicitly retain the incumbent's known deficits instead of redefining them as acceptable.

The current 5.6209% mean PNG MAE advantage of U96 over R2 is from 36 correlated observations of four photographs. It is a starting measurement, not a population estimate, recovered identity score or proof that severe privacy blur can be reversed. Native CCTV and the user's wild images have no aligned clean target and cannot supply paired accuracy measurements. A sharp plausible face cannot admit its own input. The insufficient-information threshold is calibrated from original-input judgments, with false admissions and false refusals reported; no single blur number is declared sufficient.

No guarantee is made that a larger model, higher learning rate or more epochs improves these outcomes. CodeFormer's published design explicitly combines global code prediction with a feature route controlling the fidelity/quality tradeoff. Our next experiment should therefore test the demonstrated failure and its learnable path rather than assume a favorable aggregate loss fixes it. [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.11253), [official implementation](https://github.com/sczhou/CodeFormer).
