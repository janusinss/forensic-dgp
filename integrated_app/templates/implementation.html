About this workflow

This app tries to improve a face picture. Its results are experimental and are still being reviewed. Removing a covering is an optional extra step.

Use a clearer or less-covered picture if the face is hard to see. A sharper face is not proof of identity.

Prepare the picture

Bypass keeps the original picture. You can also blur areas before improving it. This may not help every picture and can make some worse.

Mark only green to protect an area and select the rest for blur. Mark blue to blur only the areas you choose; no green is needed. If you use both, blue selects where to blur and green takes priority. With no markings, the picture stays unchanged.

Preparation is used only if the picture is improved. Off skips it, and Auto may skip it if it keeps the original. Review the preview, including already blurry areas: selected areas can become blurrier. Protected areas stay unchanged during preparation, but the generated face may still look different.
