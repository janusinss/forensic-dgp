## Concrete candidate, still unimplemented

Let `e96=A96(e)` be the exact frozen U96-adapted encoder feature at a site, `d` its current decoder input and `z=stock_SFT(e96,d,w=1)`. Propose a jointly conditioned spatial head:

`h=H(e96,d,z,coarse_e96_32)`

`z_new=z+tanh(h)*(z-d)`

At the four32/64/128/256 sites, retain the previously reviewed head-shape hypothesis: width32, projected encoder/decoder/fused/coarse context, one spatial3x3 mixing layer, and zero final projections. The old [prototype shape derivation](<C:/xampp/htdocs/YEAR 4/Testing/research/codeformer_original_spatial_correction_v1/model.py:42>) gives36 new head tensors/168,960 parameters. With U96 this would be539 frozen tensors plus36 trainable head tensors:575 total, subject to fresh actual census. The old original-only factory is incompatible and must not simply be invoked or have its original hash check waived.

This is bounded residual modulation: the elementwise correction magnitude cannot exceed `abs(z-d)`; the nominal stock-residual multiplier is between0 and2, with FP32 endpoints possible. These gates are neither sufficiency confidence nor identity probabilities. Zero final projections make the algebraic correction zero, but exact native512/RAW256/PNG parity must still be measured in the actual runtime. Small residuals and wrong frozen tokens can make this route ineffective. No gain is promised.

Use a new owned VM wrapper with one scoped lifecycle for frozen U96 pre-SFT adapters and the new post-SFT heads. The [current inference wrapper](<C:/xampp/htdocs/YEAR 4/Testing/research_spatial_restoration_v1.py:133>) explicitly forbids gradients, requires CPU and rejects pre-existing hooks; it cannot be nested unchanged with the old prototype for training. Keep decoder/fused activations connected through frozen generator operations. A blanket `no_grad` around the whole frozen backbone would also disable the new learning path. Encoder/coarse features may be detached because they are fixed input conditioning. At the first site the decoder can legitimately have no earlier-head gradient yet; do not falsely require its `requires_grad` flag to be true before inserting the first head.

For a later finite comparison, freeze identical audited inputs, complete U96 initial outputs, target/anchor definitions, objective coefficients, sampler, optimizer policy, LR and resource limits across these two owned variants:

The feature-only U96 remains an unchanged fixed incumbent comparator. Untouched CodeFormer and R2 remain declared benchmarks at returned-image review.

Both variants would use fresh objective-specific Adam state if trained; do not import feature-loss Adam moments and call that a continuation of the new objective. ## Data, objective and a bounded decision

Use genuinely detailed audited existing TRAIN sources; previously admitted clean targets do not automatically admit new degraded inputs. Generate recoverable global/local blur and paired lowlight plus clear controls, review degraded inputs alone, and freeze exclusions before outputs. The current DEV/native failure map can guide the family choice but must not become optimizer input. Preserve source terms, resolution, photo overlap limits and known uncertainty about human identity/pretraining overlap. Do not treat upsampled128px images as genuine high-resolution detail.

The primary new objective should supervise reconstructed256 RGB against the actual paired clean TRAIN target, with normalized corrupted-region and clear-context terms, plus adjacent-residual/structure disagreement. Retain brightness/signed luminance and centered residual diagnostics separately. Anchor clear/light and desired lowlight behavior to complete frozen U96 while also checking clean-target fidelity; copying U96 everywhere would preserve its defects. Do not silently substitute an original-only teacher or copy R2 spatial artifacts into dark targets. Any frozen perceptual term is optional and must earn its cost in the prospective gradient contract; no GAN or completion component is justified here.

Do not optimize the retained `delivered_rgb` helper unchanged: [its implementation](<C:/xampp/htdocs/YEAR 4/Testing/research/codeformer_spatial_continuation_v1/model.py:365>) detaches inside `no_grad`. A new optimizing path needs an explicitly connected clamp-and-resize expression with measured value agreement to actual delivery, while keeping unclipped native, differentiable delivered floats and floored PNG separate. No straight-through PNG estimator or clipping-tolerance change is silently assumed.

The next manual L4 preflight should have five explicit checks before a training packet is considered:

1. **Exact state and output zero point.** Verify R2/U96 hashes and all539 incumbent tensor contents, four adapter execution sites, new-head census, native512/RAW256/PNG and upstream token/AdaIN parity against complete U96. B freezes all539 incumbent tensors; A freezes515 and exposes the24 existing adapter tensors only for gradient measurement. Both start from identical complete U96 values. Capture signed-zero/rounding observations instead of assuming algebra proves bit equality.
2. **Real image-loss connection.** On a small fixed admitted TRAIN witness cohort, record each image/region/structure/anchor term and finite gradients to the relevant final projections. Earlier head gradients may be zero at zero initialization. A zero anchor gradient at initial U96 parity is also expected; never require all36 tensors to have nonzero gradients on the first witness.
3. **Useful bounded leverage.** Measure residual magnitudes and output sensitivity by site/role, decoder/fused graph connection and clipping. A nonzero feature gradient alone is insufficient. If the residual is effectively absent or image sensitivities cannot address the demonstrated defect, stop this hypothesis rather than silently expanding its bound or unlocking tokens.
4. **No hidden state drift.** Before any optimizer commit, demonstrate all539 incumbent values, caller RNG and teacher outputs remain exact, no stale hooks after success/controlled exception and no shared app object mutation. During later training the invariant is515 frozen tensors for A and539 for B. Save exact operation reservations and successful/failed call counters.
5. **Finite resource and follow-on contract.** Predeclare a small witness ceiling, wall/external timeout, RAM/VRAM/disk guards and output budget using fresh L4 measurements. One prospective ceiling is4 admitted TRAIN photographs x4 roles =16 inputs, up to48 full incumbent/A/B forwards and128 component-gradient queries, zero optimizer constructions/commits,20-minute external wall cap. Counts/resource feasibility must be checked and sealed before commands; these figures are not an executed packet or a measured runtime.

Only a successful returned, independently audited preflight can justify an actual-step feasibility/training pilot. This is one prospective finite experiment, not authorization to run two automatic historical recipes or append epochs after a failed stop. Actual optimizer commits are training and must be launched manually by the user on the L4 in tmux, with verified transfers, resumable new optimizer/RNG/sampler state and retained failures.
